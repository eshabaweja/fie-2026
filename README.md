# WIP: Insights from the Use of Large Language Models by Emerging Software Developers

**Accepted to IEEE Frontiers in Education (FIE) 2026, Paphos, Cyprus**

Esha Baweja, Chia-Ying Lin, Shaswatt Iswarya Sakthivel, Soorvi Prakash, Akshat Shah, Mikul Shah, Rajiv Ramnath
The Ohio State University

📄 [Read the paper (PDF)](paper.pdf)

## Summary

How do beginner programmers work differently from an LLM, and what does that mean for teaching with AI?

Five participants aged 14–22 solved 30 Python tasks of increasing difficulty while thinking aloud, and we compared their solutions with GPT-4's. Humans and the LLM differed in how they broke problems down, structured code, handled errors and debugged, and the gap grew as tasks moved from step-by-step instructions to open-ended design. LLMs work well as a reference, but used uncritically they can short-circuit the reasoning beginners need to build.
